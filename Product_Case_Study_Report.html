SECTION 01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & INDUSTRY CONTEXT

1. The Food-Delivery Blind Spot & Why This Matters

For over a decade, online food delivery platforms optimized for speed, discounts, and craving-driven discovery. While ordering food became effortless, eating genuinely nourishing food remained difficult: customers had no visibility into macronutrients (Protein, Fat, Carbs, Fibre), cooking oils, or refined ingredients when browsing restaurant menus.

Pioneered and implemented in production at Zomato (India's leading food delivery platform, under Eternal Ltd.), Healthy Mode solves this asymmetry without placing an impossible laboratory-testing burden on restaurant partners (Mx). By combining approximate preparation quantities entered by restaurants with an AI nutritional estimation engine, the platform surfaces a clear 3-tier Nutritional Heart rating (Low, Medium, High) and macro breakdown · establishing a blueprint that all food-delivery competitors should adopt to promote public health.

SECTION 02 // ALGORITHMIC ARCHITECTURE

2. The 2-Stage AI Nutritional & Healthy Score Algorithm

Stage A: Merchant (Mx) Input to AI Macro-Decomposition

Restaurant partners (Mx) provide basic preparation metadata including dish title, core ingredients, approximate gram quantities used in preparation, and cooking method (e.g., steamed, grilled, sautéed, or deep-fried). The AI pipeline maps each ingredient $i$ of weight $w_i$ (in grams) against standardized ICMR-National Institute of Nutrition (IFCT) per-100g vectors $[P_i, F_i, C_i, B_i]$ and adds a cooking-method oil absorption factor $\Delta_{\text{method}}$:

Macro_Total (g) = Σ [ (w_i / 100) × Macro_100g(i) ] + Δ_Cooking_Method
Total_Energy (kcal) = 4 × Protein(g) + 4 × Carbs(g) + 9 × Fat(g)

Stage B: The 5-Parameter Weighted Healthy Score (0–100 Scale)

Crucially, calories do not affect the Healthy Score because calories are treated as a neutral filter metric because daily energy needs vary by individual goals. Instead, the dish is evaluated on nutrient density and preparation quality:

Healthy_Score = 0.35(S_Protein) + 0.20(S_Carb_GI) + 0.15(S_Fibre) + 0.15(S_Ingredient) + 0.15(S_Cooking) − Penalty(SatFat, Sugar)
Parameter Weight Scoring Mechanics & Nutritional Rationale
1. Protein Density 35% Evaluates protein grams per serving and protein calorie share (lean meats, cottage cheese/paneer, eggs, legumes, dairy, tofu).
2. Carb Density & Quality (GI) 20% Rewards whole grains and low-glycemic index complex carbs; penalizes refined carbs unless balanced with fibre or protein.
3. Fibre & Micronutrients 15% Scores dietary fibre gram-mage and vitamin/mineral density from vegetables (broccoli, greens), fruits, legumes, herbs, and seeds.
4. Ingredient Quality 15% Rewards whole, fresh, minimally processed kitchen inputs; penalizes ultra-processed dressings or artificial additives.
5. Cooking Method 15% Steamed/poached (98) and low-oil grilled/roasted (92) score highest; deep-fried preparations (18) score lowest.
Saturated Fats & Sugar Penalty Deduction of up to −22 pts when saturated fat or added sugar exceeds healthy per-meal thresholds.
SECTION 03 // WORKED MATHEMATICAL PROOF

3. Case Example: BBQ Cottage Cheese Salad

When a restaurant partner enters a 361g serving of BBQ Cottage Cheese Salad (100g grilled cottage cheese, 115g broccoli, 110g mixed lettuce, 32g garlic bread slice, 4g herb olive dressing):

  • Derived Macros: 400 kcal (Neutral) | 23g Protein | 26g Fat | 26g Carbs | 9g Fibre
  • Weighted Score Calculation: 0.35(90.4) + 0.20(86.2) + 0.15(95.1) + 0.15(91.0) + 0.15(92.0) − 8.2 (Sat Fat Penalty) = 82.4 / 100 → High
  • Automated Positive Highlights (WHAT MAKES THIS HEALTHY): Broccoli (rich in vitamins and fibre), Cottage cheese (excellent source of protein), Mixed lettuce (hydrating and nutrient-dense).
  • Automated Nuance Tags (TO CONSIDER): Cottage cheese (contains saturated fat), Bread (potential for refined carbohydrates).
SECTION 04 // PHASE 2 PRODUCT EVOLUTION

4. From Discovery to Habit: Establishing Healthy Subscriptions

The most powerful downstream outcome of Healthy Mode is that it creates a verified, structured catalog of nutrient-dense dishes across thousands of partner kitchens. This directly unlocked Healthy Subscriptions:

1. Strict Quality Gate ( High Only)

Only dishes that achieve a High Healthy Score qualify for the subscription menu, guaranteeing consistent macro quality.

2. Multi-Day Scheduling (3 / 5 / 15 Days)

Users pre-plan Lunch (office) and Dinner (home) slots across 3, 5, or 15 days in advance, eliminating daily decision fatigue.

3. Ecosystem Flywheel for Restaurants

Predictable multi-day subscription orders reward restaurants that reformulate recipes with cleaner oils, higher protein, and whole grains.
