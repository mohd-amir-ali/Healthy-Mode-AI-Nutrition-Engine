CORE AI ALGORITHM SPECIFICATION // TWO-STAGE HYBRID ARCHITECTURE

Algorithm Name: NutriDecomp-NER + WNDS-5

Rather than relying on an unverified black-box text guess, Healthy Mode uses a Two-Stage Hybrid AI & Deterministic Biometric Scoring Pipeline combining Semantic Recipe Entity Decomposition (NutriDecomp-NER) with the 5-Parameter Weighted Nutrient Density Scoring Algorithm (WNDS-5) grounded in the ICMR-NIN (Indian Food Composition Tables – IFCT) database.

STAGE 1 AI MODEL // NUTRIDECOMP-NER + THERMAL REGRESSOR

1. Mx Prep Grams → ICMR-NIN Macro Vector

Restaurant partners (Mx) enter approximate ingredient weights (wi in grams) and cooking style. Our Food-NER Transformer maps each ingredient to its per-100g ICMR-NIN nutrient vector and adds cooking-method oil absorption (αmethod):

Macrok = ∑ [ (wi ÷ 100) × ICMR_100gi,k ] + αmethod,k
Ekcal = 4(Protein) + 4(Carbs) + 9(Fat)  [Neutral: 0% wt]
STAGE 2 SCORING ENGINE // WNDS-5 WEIGHTED CLASSIFIER

2. 5-Parameter Weighted Nutrient Density

Each dish is evaluated across five normalized biometric sub-scores (0–100) with explicit clinical weights and a threshold penalty for excess saturated fat (>7.5g) and added sugar (>6.0g):

Score = 0.35(Sprot) + 0.20(Scarb_GI) + 0.15(Sfibre)
        + 0.15(Sing) + 0.15(Scook) − Psatfat,sugar
STAGE 3 DECISION GATE // HEARTS & SUBSCRIPTIONS

3. 3-Tier Heart Mapping & NLG Insights

The composite WNDS-5 score maps deterministically to the 3-Tier Nutritional Hearts and triggers rule-based Natural Language Generation (NLG) for customer pros/cons and Healthy Subscription eligibility:

• 💚 High (≥ 72) ➔ Subscription Eligible ✓
• 💛 Medium (48 – 71.9) ➔ Balanced Everyday
• 🧡 Low (< 48) ➔ Indulgent / Occasional
WHAT IS HEALTHY SCORE?

Healthy score is calculated basis the weighted score of the dish, across the following parameters:

S = 0.35(P) + 0.20(C_GI) + 0.15(F_b) + 0.15(I_q) + 0.15(C_m) − Penalty

Protein density (35%)

Dishes with good-quality protein per portion, like lean meats, eggs, legumes, dairy or tofu score higher.

Carb density & quality (GI) (20%)

Whole grains and low-GI carbs boost the score, refined carbs can lower the score unless balanced with fibre or protein.

Fibre & micronutrients (15%)

Vegetables, fruits, legumes, herbs and seeds score higher.

Ingredient quality (15%)

Whole, fresh, and minimally processed foods score higher; processed or artificial ingredients score lower.

Cooking method (15%)

Low-oil, steamed, or grilled dishes score higher; deep-fried ones score lower.

Saturated fats & sugar (Penalty)

High saturated fats and sugar content penalise the score.

PLEASE NOTE
🔥

Calories don't affect the healthy score, as they are a neutral metric & depend on your daily intake and goals.

01 // REAL-WORLD IMPLEMENTATION AT ZOMATO (ETERNAL)

Why This Architecture Changed Food Delivery

Historically, food delivery platforms optimized purely for impulse cravings, leaving health-conscious customers guessing macro counts. By deriving structured nutritional hearts from minimal Merchant Partner (Mx) preparation quantities + AI recipe decomposition, Zomato eliminated the 20-minute menu-reading barrier without requiring small kitchens to pay for expensive laboratory food testing.

02 // PUBLIC HEALTH IMPACT & INDUSTRY CALL TO ACTION

Why Competitors Should Adopt Nutritional Transparency

Mainstreaming upfront protein, fibre, carb-quality, and ingredient commentary sends a powerful positive message to the public: ordering in does not have to compromise long-term health. When competing food-delivery platforms adopt standardized nutritional scoring, it incentivizes restaurants nationwide to reduce deep-frying oils, swap refined flour for whole grains, and unlock recurring Healthy Subscriptions.
